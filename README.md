# mental-health-classification
**CS4210: Natural Language Processing**

In this project, we aim to train models that can classify a subject’s mental status (whether they have a mental illness or not). Our main objective will be to find out whether mental illness can be accurately predicted by observing a corpus of social media posts and other subject-written text. Another objective we hope to achieve is whether or not our models are capable of classifying subjects to the same level for texts that contain specific dialects. We want to find out if we can reasonably classify different mental health disorders in texts posted on various social media sites. Additionally, we want to determine if it is easier to classify positive emotions in text than negative ones.
